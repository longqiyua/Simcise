Simcise Design System

v3.2 · Simplex × Concise · 2026.08.16
Simplex 温润Warmth first — breathing, comfort, organic rhythm. What users feel before they think.
Concise 秩序Rational order — mathematical spacing, token-driven color, systematic hierarchy. The skeleton beneath.
生长优先Elements morph in place — no destroy & recreate. A button becomes a close icon, a card unfolds.
零弹窗Never popup — every detail grows from its source card. Restraint is a feature, not a limitation.
Simplex × Concise — 温润的呼吸感 × 理性的秩序感，混合而非对立。
规范是骨架，不是枷锁。遵守 Token 和间距，其余自由发挥。

E5 Light Awareness · Global
Light has direction and warmth — surfaces respond to a virtual light source
Rule: Light comes from top-left. All surfaces show: top edge highlight (inner glow), bottom edge shadow (subtle border darkening). Consistent across every component, every product.
Surface
Radial highlight from top-left. Pure and clean.
Secondary
Warmer tone, same light direction.
Tertiary
Deeper recess, light barely reaches.
Light Formula: Every surface = base color + radial-gradient highlight (top-left 30%/20%) + 1px border (lighter top, darker bottom) + inset shadow (white highlight top, 0.5~0.7 opacity). Dark mode: reduce highlight to 0.03~0.05 opacity, increase shadow depth.
E6 Binary Star Spinner
Two stars orbiting a shared center · Overlap blends into accent light · mix-blend-mode
Rule: NOT a single ball on a track. Two circles on the same orbit, 180° apart. They revolve around the shared center. Where they cross, colors blend naturally — multiply (light) or screen (dark) creates the accent-light overlap. Use for page-level loading.

How it works: Two circles on the same orbit, 180° apart (via animation-delay: -1.4s). They revolve around the shared center. At the crossing point, mix-blend-mode: multiply blends the two accent-200 circles into a deeper accent tone — the overlap IS the accent light.
Part V · 适配 Adaptation — Concise 环境响应
30 Theme & Adaptation
Light / Dark dual mode · Responsive breakpoints · Accessibility-first
☀️
Light Mode
Warm surfaces, inset highlights, soft shadows. Ceramic warmth at full expression.
🌙
Dark Mode
Deep surfaces, accent glow intensified, shadows layered. Light awareness reduced to 3%.
♿
Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Focus-visible rings. prefers-reduced-motion respected.
Responsive: Mobile ≤ 600px (single column) · Tablet 600-1024px (2 columns) · Desktop > 1024px (full grid). Window constraints: min 360×640, recommended 1280×720+.
Motion: All animations respect @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce). When active, transitions snap to instant, breathing pauses.
